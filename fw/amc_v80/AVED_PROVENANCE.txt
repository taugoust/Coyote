LICENSE: MIT (AVED AMC) — compatible with Coyote (MIT).

Coyote-specific changes vs. upstream AVED AMC (cumulative list):

  src/profiles/v80/profile_hal.h
    - HAL_RPU_SHARED_MEMORY_BASE_ADDR : 0x38000000 -> 0x00100000
    - HAL_RPU_SHARED_MEMORY_END_ADDR  : 0x3FFFF000 -> 0x004FF000
    Reason: shared-memory region must fit inside a PCIe-visible BAR4 window,
    and Coyote's BAR4 is 256 MB total (vs AVED's larger mgmt PF aperture).
    The 4 MB region at 0x00100000 leaves room for AMC's partition table,
    ring buffer, status block, and log message buffer.

Patches should be tracked here and ideally upstreamed back to AVED once stable.
